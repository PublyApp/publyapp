## Findings

1. **Merge-blocker — O9/R2-1: retention makes bounded catch-up repeatedly resurrect pruned occurrences.** [§4.3 L742](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:742), [§5.3 L1464](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1464), [§7.3 L1855](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1855), [O9 L2252](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:2252). Reconciliation finds expected fires missing from `system_job_occurrences`, while retention deletes identities by `scheduled_fire_at` after 30 days. For `CatchUp = AtMost(n)`, the most recent missing occurrence eventually becomes a pruned old occurrence; it is re-enqueued, immediately becomes retention-eligible again, and can repeat indefinitely. Calling the identity “permanent” is also false once it is pruned. **Fix:** add a durable per-job reconciliation high-watermark, or constrain catch-up to an explicit window no longer than retention and never inspect fires at/before its lower bound. Add a spec: prune an old occurrence, run reconciliation twice, and prove it is never resurrected.

2. **Merge-blocker — §4.2/§4.5/§5.4: generic DLQ requeue discards the external-effect identity the document makes mandatory.** [§4.1 L563](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:563), [§4.2 L620](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:620), [§4.5 L833](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:833), [§5.4 L1617](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1617). An email terminal failure deletes `email_prepared_sends`; requeue then creates a new `job_id`, while the provider key is derived from `job_id`. The handler therefore re-renders mutable state and sends with a new provider key. This violates the document’s own “stable provider identity + immutable request” contract and can bypass Resend deduplication after an ambiguous accepted-without-receipt attempt. Restoring only the queue envelope is not faithful restoration for external-effect jobs. **Fix:** make `JobRegistration` define an atomic requeue policy/hook that carries the prepared bytes and original provider identity to the new job, or prohibit generic requeue for external-effect types and expose a separate, explicitly new logical-send operation. Test provider acceptance without a local receipt → DLQ → requeue, asserting identical bytes/key and no duplicate effect.

3. **Major — §10/R2-6: Phase 2C-R1 still lacks a deployable DI/wiring owner and silently depends on 2B.** [§3.2 L270](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:270), [§8 L1933](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1933), [§10 L2008](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:2008), [Phase 2C-R1 L2099](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:2099), [parallelization L2144](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:2144). R1 assumes 2B already made the dispatcher worker-only, but its heading says it depends only on 2A-R. It creates `JobQueueListener`, `IJobQueueSignal`, definitions, and handlers without assigning the required `JobsServiceRegistration.cs` edits that register the listener hosted service, signal binding, and `JobRegistration` entries. The “listener only — a new file” conflict claim is therefore false. **Fix:** distinguish parallel development from release order: `2A-R → 2B → 2C-R1` for deploys. Assign the R1 integration edit to `JobsServiceRegistration.cs`, enumerate every listener/signal/handler registration, and gate the expected registration set.

4. **Major — §5.1/R2-4: the renewal margin is not safe for the declared configuration surface and cancellation is overclaimed.** [config L225](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:225), [renewal L1144](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1144), [spec L1977](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1977). `max(2s, lease/20)` exceeds a 1–2 second lease; the current 634 validator permits `JOB_LEASE_SECONDS=1`. A fixed `lease/8` retry sleep can also cross the safety deadline unless capped by the remaining interval. Finally, cancelling a token before expiry does not prove the handler or network request stopped—cancellation is cooperative—so “never overlapping two live executions” is false. **Fix:** define and validate a minimum lease, cap every retry delay to the remaining safe interval or use a dedicated deadline timer, and state that the margin reduces overlap while external-effect idempotency remains the correctness mechanism.

5. **Major — §5.1/R2-8: the proposed Serilog “global backstop” does not sanitize the exception Serilog actually renders.** [§5.1 L1346](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1346), [L1363](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1363), [L1371](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1371). Serilog destructuring policies operate on structured property values; the special `LogEvent.Exception` remains a separate exception object that console/file sinks can render with its raw message. An `IExceptionDestructurer` therefore does not establish the claimed “anywhere” boundary. **Fix:** specify a sink/filter/formatter boundary that replaces or removes `LogEvent.Exception`, or prohibit raw exception arguments through an analyzer and log only sanitized metadata. Test the actual configured console and file output, not merely a destructured property.

6. **Major — §7/R2-10: one-alert-per-condition remains absorption by assertion.** [§7 L1780](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1780), [§7.1 L1800](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1800), [L1816](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1816), [§7.2 L1831](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:1831). Every replica emits the same breach, but a warning+ webhook sink is only a transport; no deduplication store, lease, aggregation rule, or receiver contract is specified. The table also omits `instance` from most instruments despite claiming every instrument is instance-tagged, and the “advisory-lock presence probe” has no exact non-disruptive query. **Fix:** define a concrete dedup mechanism—such as Alertmanager aggregation or a database-backed condition/window lease—plus the exact leader-presence probe and instance resource/tag convention. Test N replicas producing one notification and leader absence remaining observable.

7. **Minor — §4.6: the fold bridge state is contradictory and lacks an exact persisted value.** [§4.6 L948](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:948), [L961](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:961), [L994](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:994). 8. **Minor — §4.4: “append-only” conflicts with later reconciliation and webhook enrichment.** [§4.4 L752](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:752), [L802](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:802), [follow-up L2205](/home/radan/Projects/PublyApp/jobs-design/docs/implementation-plans/jobs-worker-infrastructure.md:2205).
